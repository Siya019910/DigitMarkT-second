Verified Plumber — JSON-LD SEO Schema Template

Local Business · Structured Data · Google Rich Results

Local service businesses — plumbers, electricians, builders — are invisible on Google despite doing great work. Their competitors rank first not because they're better, but because their websites speak Google's language. Structured data (JSON-LD) is that language, and most local businesses have none.

A reusable, production-ready JSON-LD Schema Markup template for local service businesses. Implements LocalBusiness, Service, and Review schema types — telling Google exactly who the business is, where they operate, and what customers say.

JSON-LD Schema.org HTML5 Technical SEO
  • Enables Google Rich Results (star ratings, phone number, address in search snippets) — dramatically increasing click-through rates vs. plain listings.
  • Qualifies the business for Google's Local Pack (the map + 3-result block at the top of local searches) — where 46% of all Google searches end.
  • Template is reusable across service industries — plumbers, electricians, cleaners, mechanics — any local business can be onboarded in under 30 minutes.
  • Fully Google Search Console-validated with zero structured data errors.
